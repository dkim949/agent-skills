# Explicit deep interview

Load only when the user explicitly requests a deep interview, extended questioning, or a thorough interactive stress-test. A rough idea alone uses the compact default.

## Workflow

1. State the current understanding and the unresolved choice that most threatens the outcome. Inspect relevant facts yourself. Ask about user judgment, preferences, or unavailable experience rather than discoverable facts.
2. Work through decisions in dependency order. Ask the highest-impact root question first; ask dependent questions after its answer. Independent decisions may be bundled in a supported question interface. Give a concrete situation, failure, or trade-off and a tentative recommendation with a reason when justified.
3. Update settled decisions and remaining uncertainty after replies. Challenge a vague answer such as “scalable” by asking what actual situation must work, rather than substituting a technical requirement. Explore the strongest alternative and the evidence that would change the user's mind.
4. Continue beyond three questions only while answers can materially change the decision, scope, or next test. If questioning stops producing progress, summarize the missing information and propose a cheap test or a reversible draft instead of repeating the interview.
5. Stop when the goal, included and excluded scope, binding constraints, and completion criteria are clear enough for the next action. Record important open questions and their consequence; unresolved minor details do not require exploring every branch. Stop immediately on the user's request to skip, delegate, or receive a draft, subject to existing authorization rules.

## Closure

Summarize settled decisions with reasons, material risks, the smallest falsifiable test, next actions, and remaining open questions. Deliver the requested result instead of the full conversation. Seek clarification only for a material unresolved choice; no extra shared-understanding approval is required by this mode.

This reference keeps extended interviews within the idea-refine workflow, with explicit stopping criteria and evidence-first, dependency-aware questions. It does not require a separate interview skill.
