# Red-team review

Use for an explicit request for independent critical reviewers, such as “bring a red team to review this plan” or an equivalent request in the user's language. Here red team means reviewers trying to find material failures in reasoning, evidence, design, or implementation. A brief challenge, bare “red-team this,” or ordinary review does not by itself request a team; apply the critical stance directly unless the conversation already authorizes reviewers.

1. State the proposal's strongest plausible case, intended outcome, constraints, and what would change the decision. Give reviewers the actual artifact, relevant evidence, and acceptance criteria. Avoid sending the lead's verdict or another reviewer's conclusions before their first pass. Preserve necessary context; do not obtain apparent independence by withholding a constraint that changes correctness.
2. Use `multi-agent-coordination` when installed for dispatch; otherwise use the current host's verified native tools and permissions. If delegation is unavailable, disclose that limit and give a direct critical review. Usually two read-only reviewers suffice: one challenges material assumptions, concrete counterexamples, and the strongest alternative; the other verifies important evidence and proposes the smallest check that separates competing explanations. Adapt the questions to the artifact rather than assigning decorative personas.
3. Each reviewer returns only meaningful findings: claim, evidence or exact location, concrete failure scenario, practical consequence, and mitigation or falsifiable check. Separate demonstrated failures from assumptions and checks not run. Preserve strengths that affect the recommendation. Zero material findings is valid.
4. The lead checks important findings against the artifact, source evidence, or an appropriate executable check. Merge duplicate causes. Distinguish valid defects, accepted tradeoffs, missing context, and unsupported claims. Prefer reproducible checks where possible; a plan may instead need a source-grounded contradiction or a testable unresolved assumption. Reviewer agreement, model diversity, and vote counts are not proof.
5. If a material disagreement remains, allow one focused exchange or discriminating check by default. Give the specific competing claims and evidence to the relevant reviewers; do not reopen the whole review. Stop when the decision is supported, findings repeat, or the bounded check cannot resolve the uncertainty. Report what remains unknown and the condition that would change the recommendation.

Deliver the judgment, strengths worth preserving, prioritized findings with evidence, and the next useful change or check. State whether independent reviewers actually ran and disclose any missing verification. Do not force negativity or consensus. Team review does not expand permission to edit, publish, send messages, run protected operations, change accounts, or use external providers.
